02 // QUALITY BREAKDOWN

THE ATELIER CRAFT

Crafted by hand in Germany. Every loop, shear, and binding pass is engineered for long-lasting warmth, comfort, and heirloom durability.

01

High-Density Precision Tufting

Tufted on high-tension primary backing using professional pneumatic cut-pile machinery. We load dual-strand heavyweight, ultra-soft yarns into every needle pass, creating an ultra-dense, plush pile (over 18mm depth) that feels incredible underfoot and never goes flat.

  • ✓ Dual-thread needle loading for zero bare spots
  • ✓ 100% color-fast, ultra-soft heavyweight yarn
  • ✓ Odorless German archival latex adhesive seal
02
SIGNATURE TECHNIQUE

Deep V-Groove 3D Carving

The hallmark of true textile artistry. Every color boundary is hand-carved at a 45-degree angle using precision duckbill shears and bevel trimmers. This sculpts tactile valleys between shapes, giving designs dimensional depth and crisp anime linework.

  • ✓ 4 to 8 hours of manual razor sculpting per rug
  • ✓ High-contrast shadow lines that catch ambient light
  • ✓ Clean graphic borders that pop off any floor or wall
03

40mm Cotton Köperband Binding

The gold standard of European finishing. Rather than leaving raw glued edges, we bind the entire circumference with heavy-duty 40mm matte black cotton Köperband (twill tape). Combined with premium non-slip felt underlay, it locks edges flat and prevents fraying.

  • ✓ Heavyweight 40mm cotton twill tape perimeter
  • ✓ Non-slip textured base safe for wood & marble
  • ✓ Optional reinforced brass wall-mounting brackets

04 // HANDMADE IN GERMANY

18-YEAR-OLD INDEPENDENT CREATOR. ZERO COMPROMISE.

Started in a small room with a handheld tufting gun, an obsession with anime line work, and a refusal to accept the low-grade, flat tufting that flooded social media.

At 18, I founded KONTUR in Germany to bring genuine artisanal textile rigor into streetwear and contemporary pop culture. Where commercial rug makers rush jobs in 45 minutes, I spend 6 to 12 hours individually sculpting deep V-groove reliefs and hand-binding 40mm black cotton Köperband edges.

Every rug that leaves this atelier is a signed, 1-of-1 statement piece made to bring cozy, tactile warmth to floors or hang as gallery art.

05 // COLLECTOR QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Everything you need to know about purchasing, maintaining, and commissioning custom textile art from KONTUR.

Click the 'Order via Instagram DM' button on any rug card. It opens our official Instagram page @kontur.studio5 with a pre-written message containing the dimensions and price. You can send it directly to confirm availability, provide your shipping details, and receive payment options.

Köperband is a heavyweight 40mm herringbone cotton twill tape manufactured in Germany. Instead of leaving the glued edges of the tufted cloth exposed (which leads to premature peeling and fraying), we wrap and stitch the entire back perimeter in black Köperband. This ensures museum-grade edge stability, flawless lay-flat tension, and generational durability.

Yes! We offer Tracked EU Shipping Only via DHL Express. Delivery takes 1-3 business days within Germany, and 3-5 business days across all EU member states. Every package is carefully rolled, protected, and fully insured.

Current studio turnaround is typically 10 to 14 business days from initial design approval to completion. Once shipped, you receive an immediate DHL tracking number.

Vacuum regularly using a brushless suction attachment or handheld vac (avoid spinning motorized beater bars). For spills, blot immediately with a clean, dry microfiber cloth and cold water with mild detergent. Do not machine wash or soak. Every order arrives with a printed Studio Care Guide.
